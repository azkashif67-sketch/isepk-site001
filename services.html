Enterprise IP surveillance with AI-driven analytics.

Environmental Monitoring

Protect critical assets from temperature, water and power events.

Beyond intrusion and fire, the most costly business losses often come from environmental failures — an overheating server room, a flooded basement, or a cold-chain breach. ISE PK deploys environmental monitoring that watches temperature, humidity, water ingress and power conditions around the clock, alerting your team the moment readings drift outside safe thresholds.

  • Server-room and data-centre temperature & humidity monitoring
  • Cold-chain monitoring for pharmaceuticals, food and logistics
  • Water and flood detection for basements and plant rooms
  • Power and UPS status monitoring with instant alerts
  • Automated notifications by app, SMS and email
